# OPay-Banking-Transaction-Customer-Balance-Analysis
This project analyzes banking transactions and customer balances across multiple OPay branches using Microsoft Excel. The dashboard provides insights into customer activity, transaction performance, account distribution, branch performance, and customer balances to support data-driven decision-making.
The analysis covers 2026 banking operations across four branches located in:
- Abuja
- Ibadan
- Kano
- Lagos
🎯 Business Objectives
The dashboard helps answer key business questions such as:
- Which branch processes the highest number of transactions?
- Which branch has the highest customer activity?
- How do transactions change monthly?
- Which account type is most commonly used?
- Which branch has the highest number of loan customers?
- Who are the top customers by account balance?
- Which age group holds the highest account balances?
- What is the distribution of deposits, transfers, and withdrawals across branches?

📈 Key Insights
- Ibadan recorded the highest transaction volume and emerged as the most active branch.
- Customer transactions peaked in March, indicating increased banking activity during that period.
- Current Accounts represented the largest share of customer accounts.
- Ibadan also had the highest number of loan customers.
- Customers within the 51-year age group maintained the highest combined account balances.
- A small group of customers accounted for a significant portion of total balances, highlighting opportunities for premium banking services.
- Deposit activities were generally higher than transfers and withdrawals across branches.
💡 Business Recommendations
- Increase staffing and customer service resources at the Ibadan branch to manage higher transaction volumes.
- Investigate the factors behind March's transaction spike and replicate successful campaigns.
- Develop targeted loan products for branches with lower loan adoption.
- Introduce loyalty and premium banking programs for high-value customers.
- Launch personalized financial products for age groups with strong account balances.
- Monitor branch transaction patterns regularly to improve operational efficiency.
